## Perfect version

summon.guide gives a founder one source-grounded specialist for a hard decision or a creator one cited essay-to-voice result, with installable guide agents whose counsel, skills, memory, and permissions follow the specialist without impersonation or invented sources.

### Company outcome

- **Buyer or user:** Founders and operators facing high-stakes decisions, plus creators who want a short essay turned into usable voiceover.
- **Painful problem:** Generic chat advice lacks a trusted intellectual frame, citations, continuity, and a shippable result.
- **Transformation:** A question or essay becomes sourced counsel, a reusable guide agent, or a downloadable voice asset in one session.
- **Proof that would make this undeniable:** A stranger completes a guide session, pays for the current offer, and records that the counsel or asset changed a real decision or shipped output.

## Current truth

The project reports progress 5 of 6: core loop, discovery, tracking, funnel instrumentation, and a live payment rail are in place, while stranger conversion is missing. Since that ladder was written, the repository added installable packs, OAuth member entitlements, 47 rendered distillations, a Paul Graham guide, 3,203 archived official-source pages, and an agent-first guide registry. The commercial documents conflict: `NORTH_STAR.md` says $10 for 100 messages, `OFFER.md` says $29, and the membership layer introduces another entitlement model.

### Verified evidence

- Streaming chat, compare mode, source citations, `/speak`, and MP3 download are implemented.
- `checkout_click` is captured through PostHog and Vercel tracking on paywall surfaces.
- The member layer includes OAuth, entitlement checks, five-session allowance, and Stripe webhook support in source.
- Forty-seven one-page distillations render; four official-source archives total 3,203 pages with zero remaining sync failures at the recorded checkpoint.
- `EVIDENCE.md` records no paid customer receipt or stranger revenue number.
