# Sam Walton: listening and cost discipline

## Key lessons

- Walmart publishes Walton rules emphasizing employee participation, communication, listening and customer expectations.
- Cost control supports competitive resilience. Application: ask frontline staff about one avoidable expense and test a service-preserving improvement. This adaptation does not establish that every cost cut is beneficial.
