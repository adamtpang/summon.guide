# Gottman Institute: Turn Towards Instead of Away

Original synthesis of a public Gottman Institute article; not a transcript or quotation. Reviewed 2026-09-22.

Source: https://www.gottman.com/blog/turn-toward-instead-of-away/

Notice and respond to everyday attempts to connect.

- A bid may be a request for attention, affection, acknowledgement, or practical help. Pay attention to context rather than assuming you know the hidden meaning.
- Discuss what makes each person feel noticed. One missed bid does not diagnose the relationship or prove rejection.
