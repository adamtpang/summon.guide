# Gottman Institute: The Gottman Method

Original synthesis of a public Gottman Institute article; not a transcript or quotation. Reviewed 2026-09-22.

Source: https://www.gottman.com/about/the-gottman-method/

Friendship, managing conflict, and shared meaning are connected areas of relationship work.

- John and Julie Gottman jointly developed the Sound Relationship House framework. Knowing a partner’s inner world and expressing appreciation support connection.
- The framework distinguishes recurring differences from solvable problems. A recurring disagreement is not automatically evidence of incompatibility.
- Trust, commitment, and space for each person’s aspirations matter alongside everyday communication. These educational notes do not constitute assessment or therapy.
