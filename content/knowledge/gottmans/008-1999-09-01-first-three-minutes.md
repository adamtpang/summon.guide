## Key lessons

- Situation: Carrere and Gottman tested whether the first minutes of a conflict conversation predict divorce. They coded the marital conflict discussion of 124 newlywed couples with the Specific Affect Coding System, totaling positive and negative affect in five 3-minute blocks.
- What they found: marital outcome over a 6-year period could be predicted from just the first 3 minutes for both husbands and wives. For husbands the prediction improved as groups diverged over the remaining 12 minutes; for wives it stayed about as strong throughout.
- Boundaries: the abstract reports predictability in this sample of newlyweds, not a cross-validated forecast, and it says nothing about what to do differently.
- Transferable lesson: the opening line of a difficult talk matters. Starting with blame or contempt sets the tone, so the practical takeaway is to begin gently and specifically, and to notice how you start, not to fear a three-minute verdict.
