# Gottman Institute: Soften Your Start-Up

Original synthesis of a public Gottman Institute article; not a transcript or quotation. Reviewed 2026-09-22.

Source: https://www.gottman.com/blog/softening-startup/

Raise a specific concern without attacking your partner’s character.

- Describe the situation, your feeling, and a concrete request. An accusation disguised as an I-statement is still an accusation.
- Use a manageable current issue rather than a catalogue of past failures. The goal is an opening the other person can hear, not suppressing the concern.
