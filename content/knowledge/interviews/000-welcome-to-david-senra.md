# Welcome to David Senra

> **What the show is:** An 81-second launch trailer in which David Senra introduces his new interview podcast, a companion to Founders, built around long conversations with the best living founders in the world.

*Trailer by David Senra. [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qK1Yvt24WYg) · 1 min · published 2025-09-24.*

## Key lessons

- **It extends Founders from history to the present.** Senra says he launched Founders nine years earlier to tell the stories of history's greatest entrepreneurs; this new show sits down with living founders instead.
- **The guest list sets the bar.** The trailer names early guests including Daniel Ek, Michael Dell, Brad Jacobs, Todd Graves and Michael Ovitz.
- **The mission is unchanged.** Senra frames everything he does as obsessively studying the greats to find timeless ideas listeners can apply in their own work.
- **The cadence.** Founders continues weekly, and the new show releases an episode every other Sunday on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, X and other platforms.
- **The tone.** Short, unattributed clips from guests stress never being satisfied, liking pressure, and treating failure as not an option.
