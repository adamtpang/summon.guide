## Key lessons

- Situation: she describes San Lorenzo, a Rome tenement district of overcrowded, dark, sublet apartments, where poor children grew up with little light, water, or privacy.
- What she argued: children cannot be educated in isolation from their surroundings. A housing association cut large apartments into small family homes, gave tenants responsibility for upkeep, and put a school inside the building as the community's shared property.
- Concrete details: the school took children too young for elementary school so that working mothers could leave them safely. Mothers were expected to send children clean and to talk with the teacher weekly. She was writing about one reform effort in one district.
- Lesson: before fixing a child's behavior, look at the conditions the child lives in, and give parents a real stake in how children are cared for. Caution: her tone toward the poor is paternalistic by today's standards, and the duties placed on mothers reflect that.
