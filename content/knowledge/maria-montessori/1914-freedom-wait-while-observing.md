## Key lessons

- Situation: in her short book for families, she explains how an adult should guide small children without getting between the child and their experience.
- What she argued: a lesson in the ordinary sense cools the child's enthusiasm. Keep an attitude of respect, calm and waiting. Her motto for the educator is "Wait while observing." Adults should share the child's joys and difficulties and show patience with slow progress.
- Concrete claims: children choose their own work, persist in it, and seek effort rather than avoid it. She notes that adults often force children to follow them and are rude, then expect submission while children imitate them anyway.
- Lesson: treat children with the courtesy you want for yourself. She defines kindness not as caresses but as interpreting others' wishes and conforming to them, sometimes at the cost of your own preference.
