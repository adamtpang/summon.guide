# Marie Curie: research and protection

## Key lessons

- The historical account discusses Curie establishing laboratory protection measures and concerns about radium used by unqualified practitioners.
- Application: pair a research plan with qualified safety review. Do not turn her biography into instructions for handling radioactive material.
