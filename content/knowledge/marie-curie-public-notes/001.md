# Marie Curie: investigate an unexplained measurement

## Key lessons

- The AIP history explains that pitchblende emitted more radiation than its known uranium content accounted for, prompting investigation of another element.
- Application: record the mismatch, competing explanations and a discriminating test. Do not apply hazardous historical laboratory practices.
