# albert-einstein

Original Summon syntheses, written 2026-10-01.

- Source: (1) Relativity: The Special and General Theory, by Albert Einstein (1916; this text marked revised edition 1924), authorised translation by Robert W. Lawson (Methuen, 1920), read as plain text from Project Gutenberg ebook 30155 (https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/30155/pg30155.txt), saved as _src-relativity.txt. (2) Sidelights on Relativity, by Albert Einstein (1922), containing 'Ether and the Theory of Relativity' (Leyden, 5 May 1920) and 'Geometry and Experience' (Berlin, 27 January 1921), read as plain text from Project Gutenberg ebook 7333 (https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/7333/pg7333.txt), saved as _src-sidelights.txt. The Gutenberg text does not name the translator of Sidelights.
- Read in full: no
- Parts read: Relativity: Preface and Sections I to XXIII read in full (Part I complete, Part II through 'Behaviour of Clocks and Measuring-Rods on a Rotating Body of Reference'). Sidelights on Relativity: both lectures read in full, start to end.
- Parts not read: Relativity: Sections XXIV to XXXII and Appendices I to IV were not read. Appendix V is not in the Gutenberg text (still under copyright, per the ebook's own note). Formulas in the Relativity text appear only as image placeholders and were not seen. No note draws on any unread part.

## Caveats

- The plain-text URL for ebook 5001 returned a 404 page, so Relativity was read from ebook 30155 (same work, same Lawson translation). Notes 001 to 005 therefore use https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/30155 as youtube_url, not 5001. Change to 5001 if the pipeline requires it.
- A leftover file _t.tmp in the folder holds the HTML of ebook 5001 from a download attempt. It is not a source that was read and can be removed; it was left because the brief says not to delete anything.
- Both works are English translations of German originals; the short quotations are the translators' wording, not Einstein's German.
- Note 001 rests on a preface of about 25 lines, so its bullets are fine-grained.
- Note 004 (Section IX) refers back to Sections VI, VII and VIII, which were also read in full.
- Note 004 contains one 'Applied today' bullet that is this synthesis's application, not Einstein's statement.
- Some physics in these texts is dated: the 1921 lecture's suggestion that space is probably approximately spherical and the 1920 lecture's view that elementary particles are condensations of the electromagnetic field are Einstein's positions at the time, reported as such.
- The 'as far as the laws of mathematics refer to reality' passage was seen verbatim in the Gutenberg text of Geometry and Experience; only a six-word fragment is quoted.

No source text is stored here. Every lesson is a paraphrase.
