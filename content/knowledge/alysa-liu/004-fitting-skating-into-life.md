## Key lessons

- Liu told Team USA that after Beijing 2022 she no longer wanted to skate. She said she had no goals and felt she was training and competing for no reason.
- She said her life had revolved around the rink and, as a kid, she did not want to be there all day, every day.
- She said the pandemic shutdown was her first real break, and it showed her that a break was what she wanted.
- Away from the sport she found she enjoyed volleyball, tennis and basketball, and creative work such as costume design. She describes herself as someone who likes turning her own artistic ideas into something visible.
- She stated the reversal directly: she no longer fits her life into skating, she fits skating into her life.
- The profile reports she won her first competition back (the Budapest Challenger event), placed second at the U.S. Championships, and won gold at the 2025 World Championships.
- Liu said winning and losing no longer affect her, that medals do not fulfill her, and that she skates because she likes to.
- She said she used to feel disconnected from her programs because she had not created them. Now she is proud of them and wants to share them, without seeking outside approval.
- She said she would love to go to the 2026 Olympics, but that her return was not driven by medals or rankings. In her words, "Every day I choose to be on the ice."
- Applied today: if you return to something demanding, decide in advance which parts you will author yourself, because ownership of the work was what she says changed her relationship to it.
