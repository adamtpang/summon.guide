## Key lessons

- CNN reports Liu won the 2025 world title in Boston, the first for a US woman in 19 years, ahead of three-time defending champion Kaori Sakamoto. Liu noted she had not yet been training for a full year.
- She said the event was emotional and bittersweet because Worlds had also been her last competition before retiring.
- Looking back at 2022, Liu said she was a kid who wanted ordinary human moments, and that she had never taken a vacation until after she quit.
- She said she felt isolated while traveling constantly for training. She accepted it was necessary with an Olympics close, but found it very hard.
- She described the strain of other people wanting a gold medal from her while she wanted something else, and said that meant she was not doing it for herself.
- She said people kept telling her who she was, and she had never had the space to work that out on her own.
- Liu agreed she had come to hate parts of the sport. At the time she believed the break would be permanent.
- During the break she said she avoided rinks entirely and did not talk about or watch skating. A ski trip to Lake Tahoe with friends brought back the cold, the adrenaline and the physical effort, and she decided to try the ice again because it was easier to reach than the mountains.
- She summed up where she landed as "a really healthy medium," and said she was excited about her own growth heading toward the 2026 Olympics.
