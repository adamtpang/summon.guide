## Caveats

- Sources disagree on her Beijing 2022 placement: the 2022 and 2024 U.S. Figure Skating pieces and Team USA say seventh, the 2026 U.S. Figure Skating piece says sixth. Each note reports what its own source states.
- The 2024 U.S. Figure Skating article refers to a bronze at the '2024 World Championships'; the other sources date that bronze to 2022. The note does not repeat the 2024 date.
- Sources differ slightly on the ski trip (Team USA calls it a family trip, CNN says with friends at Lake Tahoe) and on the length of the break (about a year and a half off the ice, two seasons out of competition).
- Her 2022 account (goals met, graduating) and her 2025 accounts (no goals, isolation, came to hate parts of it) differ in tone. Both are her own words at different times and are reported separately, not reconciled.
- Deliberately left out though present in the sources: details about her father and family history, a federal case involving her family, a remark about getting sick from travel, and her comments on a 2025 plane crash. These fall outside the public professional record the guide is limited to.
- Notes 005 and 006 include a coach's and a writer's characterizations; these are labeled as theirs, not hers.
- Raw downloaded pages (_page-*.html, _page-*.txt) and two helper scripts remain in the output folder because the brief forbids deleting; they are working files, not notes.

No source text is stored here. Every lesson is a paraphrase.
