## Key lessons

- U.S. Figure Skating reports Liu won the women's title at Milano Cortina 2026 with a free skate of 150.20 and a total of 226.79, rising from third after the short program. It describes this as the first Olympic gold for a US woman since 2002, and her second gold of the Games after the team event.
- This article gives her 2022 Beijing placement as sixth, where earlier U.S. Figure Skating and Team USA pieces say seventh.
- Liu said she was happy with how she skated and that she felt connected with the audience. She said she wants to be out there again.
- Asked what the journey means, she said it is about doing things people tell you that you should not do, and that she has done a lot of that.
- She added a condition: you also have to find a good team. She thanked the support around her and her friends.
- She said she wants to be a storyteller and is glad more people are watching so she can show what she has come up with.
- In an earlier interview with U.S. Figure Skating quoted in the piece, she said she is not really a skater and feels more like an artist, and that her decisions make sense once her actual goals are understood.
- Before competing in Milan she told NBC, as quoted here, that she is only there because she likes it. She said "It would be a problem if I didn't have joy."
- The article says she retired in 2022 to explore other interests, including travel and study at UCLA, and returned for the 2024-25 season, winning the 2025 World Championships and the 2025 Grand Prix Final.
