## Key lessons

- Liu said she had only two goals in skating: to compete at an Olympics, and to enjoy the journey with her skating friends. Once both were met in 2022 she decided there was nothing further she wanted from the sport.
- She told U.S. Figure Skating that if reaching those goals had taken another four-year cycle, she would have stayed longer. The stopping point was tied to the goals, not to an age or a result.
- She named the costs plainly: the grind of training, the stress of competing, and the time away from the people close to her. She said she was choosing those people over skating.
- She described the decision as feeling less like a sacrifice and "more like I'm graduating."
- Liu said she made the decision after the Beijing Olympics in February 2022, where the article reports she finished seventh, but still chose to skate the World Championships in March as a last competition. The article reports she won bronze there.
- She said she skated that final event very emotionally and did not expect to do well because of it.
- Asked about the surprised reaction to her announcement, she said she had not expected one and did not much care, and that she preferred not knowing what people were saying.
- At the time she said she did not think she would return to competition. Her stated plans were ordinary ones: ice shows that spring, a driver's license, college applications for fall 2023, and more time at the piano, which skating had crowded out.
- Applied today: before leaving a demanding pursuit, it can help to write down what you originally wanted from it and check honestly whether those things have already happened.
