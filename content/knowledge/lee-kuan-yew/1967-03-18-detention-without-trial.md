## Key lessons

- To a room of lawyers he defended detention without trial under the Criminal Law (Temporary Provisions) Ordinance, and did not pretend it fit liberal legal principle.
- He called it a deviation: "we have had to adjust, to temporarily deviate from, ideals and norms."
- This is a cost a modern reader should weigh critically: detainees were held because evidence could not convict them in open court. The guide must present it as a cost, never as a model to admire.
