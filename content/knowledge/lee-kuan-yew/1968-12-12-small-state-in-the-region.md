## Key lessons

- He opened by noting that an indiscreet word in New York could bring rapid rejoinders in Southeast Asia, 10,000 miles away.
- A small state lacks the weight to absorb the cost of being misquoted about bigger neighbours or powers, so he chose care over blunt commentary.
- Calibrate candor to what you can afford to have repeated.
