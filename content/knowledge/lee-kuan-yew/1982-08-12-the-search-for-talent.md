## Key lessons

- Asked what most drove Singapore's development since 1959, he answered without hesitation: the quality of the people, not resources or geography.
- Of the first seven in Singapore's protocol order at independence, only he was born in Singapore; he stated this plainly rather than hide it.
- Finding, importing and keeping talent is a permanent first-order job of any institution, not an occasional recruitment exercise.
