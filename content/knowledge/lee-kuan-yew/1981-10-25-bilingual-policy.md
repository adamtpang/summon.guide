## Key lessons

- The 1980 census showed 67 percent of Singaporeans aged five and over spoke a Chinese dialect at home, 6 percent Mandarin, 6 percent English.
- He called shifting the Chinese community to Mandarin probably the most difficult task of 22 years of PAP government.
- He treated language as a constraint on children's cognitive load, two languages instead of three or four, not as an identity project.
