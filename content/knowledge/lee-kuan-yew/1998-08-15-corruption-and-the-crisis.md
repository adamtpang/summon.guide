## Key lessons

- In the middle of the Asian Financial Crisis he pointed to neighbouring economies where corruption and nepotism turned a regional shock into collapse.
- Singaporeans could watch the counterexample happen next door, which taught the lesson more durably than any speech about honesty.
- Honest institutions are not a moral decoration on top of economics; in a crisis they are the difference.
