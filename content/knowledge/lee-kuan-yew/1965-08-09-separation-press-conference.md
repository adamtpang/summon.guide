## Key lessons

- Hours after Singapore's expulsion from Malaysia became final, he explained it live on camera instead of waiting for a polished version of himself.
- He let the difficulty show, breaking down mid-answer, then continued; his authority came from visible accountability under uncertainty, not from projecting invulnerability.
- Days later at Serangoon Gardens he framed the new country as something to keep and defend together: "It is mine: I keep it; I defend it".
