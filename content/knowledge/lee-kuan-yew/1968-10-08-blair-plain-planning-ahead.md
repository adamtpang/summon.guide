## Key lessons

- The estate took five years from plan (early 1963) to opening; he named that time lag as a danger in all planning.
- He urged "always being ahead of your problems, because there is a time-lag between the formulation of a solution and the actual implementation."
- A still-high birth rate meant housing could be outrun by demand before it was built, so he tied the opening to family planning.
