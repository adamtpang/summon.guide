## Key lessons

- Singapore's forces grew from two infantry battalions in 1965 to an army, navy and air force in sixteen years, starting with almost no doctrine of their own.
- They borrowed doctrine and training from the Israelis, British, Americans, Australians and New Zealanders, then modified it for local terrain, climate and manpower.
- National pride was never allowed to block copying a better answer; disciplined local adaptation followed immediately.
