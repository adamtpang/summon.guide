## Key lessons

- A visible creator can attract contributors who want to help without becoming the public performer themselves. Visa frames creative achievement partly as collaboration, not only individual skill. [1:01–2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r80xzfvtmVs&t=61s)
- Explain where the work is heading, not merely how it differs from competitors today. Direction gives interested people a journey they can follow. [2:34–4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r80xzfvtmVs&t=154s)
- Making work legible to its intended audience need not mean diluting it for an audience you do not want. Clarify the fit between what you make and whom it serves. [4:04–4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r80xzfvtmVs&t=244s)
- Help others understand how to engage with the work, and cultivate relationships in the field. Consider the audience's experience and explain what is valuable rather than assuming quality will be self-evident. [6:36–7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r80xzfvtmVs&t=396s), [8:08–9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r80xzfvtmVs&t=488s)
