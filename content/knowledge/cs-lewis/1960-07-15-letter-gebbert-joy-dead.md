## Key lessons

- Situation: two days after Joy died on 13 July 1960, Lewis wrote a short note to a long-time American correspondent who had been sending gifts "for the three of us".
- What he did: he stated the death in one sentence, then said they had hoped until within about ten days of the end that Joy would hold her own.
- He named one merciful fact: the cancer had reached the spine and could have meant days of suffering, so he could not wish that she had lived.
- He also set a boundary, saying he had no heart to write more and hoped his next letter would be less depressing.
- Transferable lesson: in the first days, brief and honest is enough. Naming one merciful fact does not cancel the grief, and asking for space is legitimate.
