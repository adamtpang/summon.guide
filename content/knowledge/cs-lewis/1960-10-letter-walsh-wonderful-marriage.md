## Key lessons

- Situation: three months after Joy's death Lewis wrote to Walsh, an American professor and early writer on his work, who had shared his sadness.
- What he did: he thanked Walsh for seeing that their happiness had added something to Walsh's own, then described the marriage as wonderful, even at the end.
- He recorded two of Joy's last sentences, thanking him for making her happy and saying she was at peace with God, and remarked that many would wish to end life that way.
- Transferable lesson: tell the good part of the story out loud to someone who loved the person. It does not replace the pain, but it keeps the whole relationship in view.
- Quote: "It was a wonderful marriage." (Lewis to Walsh, October 1960, as quoted on the linked page.)
