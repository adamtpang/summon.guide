## Key lessons

- Situation: a run of letters to one American friend over seven years tracks the arc, from a possible deathbed marriage on 16 November 1956 to the relapse in October 1959 and the note after Joy died on 15 July 1960.
- What he did: he wrote briefly and honestly. In November 1956 he said he might soon be "a bridegroom and a widower". In October 1959 he said the 1957 recovery had been only a reprieve. After the death he described himself as "like a sleep-walker".
- Late in life, on 17 June 1963, he counselled her to stop resisting: rest, let go, trust what is underneath. He observed that we often become afraid because we struggle.
- Transferable lesson: what Lewis learned from losing Joy shows in how he later comforted others, with calm, practical language and no attempt to argue the grief away.
