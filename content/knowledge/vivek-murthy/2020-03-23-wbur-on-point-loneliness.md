## Key lessons

- Situation: recorded as COVID-19 distancing began, Murthy warned of a possible social recession, a rise in loneliness and its health effects as people became more isolated.
- What he recommended: spend at least 15 minutes a day talking or writing to someone you love; focus on the quality of that time and remove distraction, since multitasking dilutes a call until you barely remember it; and use service (helping a neighbor, advising a colleague, or simply listening), because giving help reminds us of our value and purpose.
- Concrete fact he cited: by his account more than 20% of US adults admitted struggling with loneliness then, more than have diabetes. Later official documents use higher figures (see the 2023 advisory), so numbers vary by survey and year.
- Transferable lesson: start tiny and consistent. He said simply showing up and being present is a lost art, and that technology can help if used to deepen attention rather than replace it.
