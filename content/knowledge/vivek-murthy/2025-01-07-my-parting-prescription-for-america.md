## Key lessons

- Situation: as his second term ended, Murthy wrote a personal essay. It opens with his father's remark that he never felt emptiness until he left his village in India, and with parents who modeled showing up for others.
- What he argued: community is the missing ingredient. Three elements make it: relationships, service, and purpose, with love as the core virtue. He contrasts this triad of fulfillment with the modern triad of success (wealth, fame, power) and says the second cannot fill the gap.
- Concrete actions: reach out daily even briefly; create technology free zones for conversations; commit to one act of kindness a day; find purpose in contributing to others. He notes around a third of adults and half of young people experience loneliness.
- Transferable lesson: his closing wish is "Choose community." (Murthy, 2025). Treat needing others as human, not weak. Rights note: US government publication; this PDF is a third party copy and the canonical file is on hhs.gov.
