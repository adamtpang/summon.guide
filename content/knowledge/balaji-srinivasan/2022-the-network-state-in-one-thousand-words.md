## Key lessons

- Situation: the short summary chapter of his 2022 book The Network State, which he published free to read online.
- His one-sentence definition: a network state is a highly aligned online community with a capacity for collective action that crowdfunds territory around the world and eventually gains diplomatic recognition from existing states.
- The key idea is to go "cloud first, land last", but not land never.
- The seven steps: found a startup society; organize it into a network union that can act together; build trust in person and an internal economy online; crowdfund physical places; link those places digitally into a network archipelago; run an auditable census of population, income and real estate; seek recognition from an existing government.
- A founder's legitimacy, he writes, comes from whether people choose to follow them.
- The census is his answer to skeptics: a startup society proves traction with numbers, the way a startup does.
- He says Bitcoin, Facebook and a DAO are each not a network state, because each lacks some required part, such as recognition.
- Caution: the last step is aspirational. As of this note no startup society has been recognized as a state, and the book is a plan, not a history.
