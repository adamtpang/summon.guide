## Key lessons

- Situation: an essay first published on 1729.com on 9 April 2021 and later carried on his own site. It became the seed of his 2022 book.
- Why he wants new countries: for the same reason people want a blank page or a fresh startup, to "build something new without historical constraint". He argues that when a fresh start is impossible, people fight over the old.
- He lists six known routes and sets them aside: election (too crowded), revolution and war (he says plainly he does not advise attempting either), micronations (too few people care), seasteading (no working example yet) and space.
- His seventh route is the cloud country: start with a digital community, organize it, then crowdfund territory in the physical world over time.
- He notes a state is a social thing, like a currency: a few people on a remote platform cannot get recognized, so the community has to come before the land.
- Transferable lesson for any new project: when reform of an existing institution is blocked, ask what the smallest fresh start is that people would voluntarily join.
- Caution: this is a proposal, not a record of something achieved. No network state has gained diplomatic recognition.
