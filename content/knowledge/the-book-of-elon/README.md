## Caveats

- The book is a compilation of Musk's statements from transcripts, tweets, and interviews, edited and recontextualized by Jorgenson. The compiler's own note says he cannot be certain every source is authentic and asks readers to verify phrasing against a primary source before citing Musk. Notes therefore attribute every claim to Musk (he says, he argues) and should not be cited as verified fact or as exact Musk quotations.
- All figures (costs, percentages, dates, odds) are Musk's own as given in the book and were not independently checked. Many Part IV items (robots, AI timelines, Mars cargo dates such as 2026, 2028, 2044, Tesla valuation) are forecasts, not outcomes.
- Internal inconsistencies in the book: the PayPal sale to eBay is given as about $4.5 billion in the Part III text but $1.5 billion in the Timeline (the Timeline figure matches the widely reported price); PayPal proceeds after tax appear as about $180 million in the text and about $170 million in the Timeline; one passage dates the PayPal sale to 2001 while others say 2002. Note 006 avoids stating a sale price for this reason.
- The book is one-sided by design: it presents Musk's views on management (long hours, firing, distrust of camaraderie), regulation, media, climate, population, and AI without counterargument, and the compiler states it covers none of his family life or politics. Contested claims (for example on overpopulation, nuclear versus coal deaths, and AI political correctness) are reported as his opinions.
- The five-step Algorithm is covered in full in note 004. The book words step one as making requirements less dumb; the bonus list restates it as question requirements.
- The file is an automated PDF-to-Markdown conversion with running headers, split words, and a few garbled characters; section names in locators follow the table of contents. Page numbers are not used as locators because they are unreliable in the conversion.
- Each note has at most one short quotation, under ten words, checked against the text as read.

No source text is stored here. Every lesson is a paraphrase.
