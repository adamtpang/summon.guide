## Key lessons

- Situation: once his early symptoms cleared, Cornaro asked whether the popular proverb that whatever pleases the palate must suit the body was true.
- What he did: he observed his own reactions and concluded the proverb was false for him, since several foods and drinks he loved reliably disagreed with him. He then adjusted according to what he saw.
- Lesson: pay attention to cause and effect in your own life, and let evidence overrule habit and social custom, which he calls "a second nature".
- Caution: his particular list reflects one person's digestion and 16th century ideas. It is not a guide to what anyone else should eat or avoid.
