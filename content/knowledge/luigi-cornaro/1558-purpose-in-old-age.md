## Key lessons

- Situation: he describes his days at what he claims is age eighty-three, against the common view that life after seventy-five is death rather than life.
- What he did: he lists his pleasures, including conversation with friends, reading and writing to be of use, tending gardens, visiting his hillside lodge and villa, following the draining of marshland he had backed, and playing with his grandchildren.
- Lesson: his contentment came from purpose, company and beauty, not from a rule. He also says he wrote a comedy at that age, a claim we cannot verify.
- Caution: this is written to sell his method, and he was a wealthy landowner with leisure that most readers will not share.
