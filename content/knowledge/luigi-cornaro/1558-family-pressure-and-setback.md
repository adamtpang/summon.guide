## Key lessons

- Situation: in his own late seventies, his family and physicians worried he was doing too little and urged a change. He gave in, he says, to keep the peace.
- What he did: he made a modest change to his routine, and within days reports feeling peevish and low, then a long fever. He credits his earlier steady habits for his recovery.
- Lesson: he tells this as a caution about dropping what works because others insist. The deeper point is that a change to a routine deserves a reason and a check on how you actually feel.
- Caution: he read his illness as proof of his method, which is unproven. Do not use his story to resist medical advice; it is one man's account.
