## Key lessons

- Situation: by his own account, Cornaro reached his mid to late thirties in bad health from years of overindulgence, with stomach pain, gout symptoms, fever and constant thirst. Treat this as his self-report.
- What he did: his physicians told him a plain, regular life was the only path left. He admits he had heard this before and ignored it, and had hidden his drinking from them. When he finally committed, he says he stayed with it and felt better within days.
- Lesson: the turning point was not new information but a decision to stop fighting advice he already had. Change begins when evidence and discomfort finally outweigh comfort.
- Caution: this is a man reporting on himself, and his stated ages are known to be inflated. Anyone with symptoms like his needs a doctor, not a 16th century memoir.
