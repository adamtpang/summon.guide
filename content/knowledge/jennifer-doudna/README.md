## Caveats

- Living person: notes cover the public professional record only. Family details in the sources (marriage, child, a parent's illness) were left out, except her father's role in her path into science, which she describes herself.
- Note 004 is a multi-author paper; its positions are those of all the Napa authors, not Doudna alone. The paper does not use the word moratorium, although Doudna's biographical statement and the Issues interview later describe it that way.
- Note 007: the CRISPR patent dispute is reported only as far as the interview covers it (the 2022 US Patent Office ruling and her reaction). The framing of who won is the interviewer's. The page may be partly paywalled, so the saved text may omit later questions.
- Note 008 is the blog's edited highlights of a live chat, not a full transcript; one or two question headings were missing in the saved page.
- Note 005 is a short statement (seven bullets cover nearly all of it).
- The Nobel biographical statement is self-authored and retrospective; dates and credit assignments are her own account.
- One short quotation per note, each seen in the fetched text. A final scripted check for stray dashes and quote matches was blocked by a local shell guard, so those checks were done by eye.
- Working files (_page-N.html, _page-N.txt, _lecture.pdf, _lecture.txt, _strip.py) remain in the folder and are not notes.
