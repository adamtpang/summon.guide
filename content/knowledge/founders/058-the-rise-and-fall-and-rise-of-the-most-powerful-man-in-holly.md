# The Rise and Fall (and Rise) of the Most Powerful Man in Hollywood: Michael Ovitz

> **Founder principle (Michael Ovitz):** An intermediary becomes valuable by creating opportunities that would not otherwise exist. Ovitz combined exhaustive research, shared client service, and complete film packages to move power toward CAA and its clients. His later conflicts show that commercial success does not settle questions of trust, ownership, or whether a new role actually gives you room to operate.

## Key lessons

- **Turn junior access into an education nobody assigns you.** In the William Morris mail room, Ovitz studied the firm's old client files before and after work, brought questions to its legal staff, and made himself useful to a senior executive at night. The assignment was modest, but access to contracts, decisions, and experienced people let him learn the business far beyond that assignment. Identify the records and practitioners your current position lets you study, then build a deliberate apprenticeship around them.
- **Choose early customers partly for how quickly they pay.** CAA began with borrowed money, makeshift furniture, and partners' wives helping answer the phones. Television writers and actors brought dependable weekly payments while film representation offered slower, less certain rewards. A young service business needs a route to cash that keeps it alive long enough to pursue its larger ambition.
- **Make the whole firm responsible for the client outcome.** Ovitz wanted CAA's agents to share clients rather than defend individual territories. The same approach supported assembling writers, directors, and actors into packages that reduced a studio's development burden. Design incentives and working practices around completing the customer's project, especially where separate specialists would otherwise leave the coordination undone.
- **Earn a prospect's attention by bringing a concrete opportunity.** Ovitz pursued a literary agent with a scheduled weekly call for a year before receiving a novel to adapt. CAA also got Shogun made after it had spent years with another agency. Prepare useful material and follow through on neglected projects so that a prospective client can judge demonstrated work instead of a sales promise.
- **Expand through the underlying skill, then study the new domain.** Ovitz saw parallels between assembling a film and bringing parties together in a corporate acquisition. He learned from deal specialists, studied the Panasonic founder's writings and products, and examined Coca-Cola's advertising history before pursuing unfamiliar assignments. The transferable skill opened a door, but extensive preparation made the expansion credible.
- **Revisit ownership and expectations before resentment becomes permanent.** Ovitz's partners accepted his request for a larger equity share, yet his retrospective account describes lasting emotional damage beneath the agreement. He also misread his closest partner's concealed financial troubles and grievances. Treat a negotiated settlement as the start of an ongoing relationship check, and do not assume agreement proves that ambitions, risks, and perceived contributions remain aligned.
- **Evaluate authority separately from friendship and prestige.** Before joining Disney, Ovitz saw reporting arrangements change and heard warnings about Michael Eisner's treatment of deputies. He nevertheless expected their friendship to make the arrangement work, then found his initiatives repeatedly blocked. Before accepting a role, establish who can approve decisions and how disagreements will be resolved, particularly when your past success depended on independence.
- **Reduce intensity when its personal cost exceeds its business value.** Ovitz describes hundreds of daily calls, working dinners, and children who noticed that his attention was elsewhere even when he was present. Looking back, he believed he could have worked less after the business gained strength without sacrificing its success. Reassess the schedule as the company matures, because a pace that once secured survival can later consume time that cannot be recovered.
