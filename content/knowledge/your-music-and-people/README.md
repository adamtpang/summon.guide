## Caveats

- The file is a PDF text extraction stored as a few very long lines, so a per-call size cap blocked the Read tool on the body. The header and contents were read with Read; the body was read in full by printing consecutive character ranges to the terminal. Nothing was copied to disk.
- The book has no chapter numbers. Locators give the section name and chapter title. Chapter titles are written with plain apostrophes and without the book's quotation marks.
- Eleven sections are covered in seven notes: Intro with Creative, Describe with Target, and Quantity with Money and Mindset are grouped. Note 007 is therefore the most compressed.
- Statistics and anecdotes (375 of 500 stations, CD Baby averages, TV audience shares, placebo and quiz experiments, the Tony Robbins fee, the pop star meeting 50 people a day) are Sivers's own unsourced claims and are reported as such, not independently verified.
- The chapter Never wait relays one lawyer's conference remark about selling a cover song without a publisher's reply. It is reported neutrally and is not legal advice.
- The chapter Show success before asking for help includes Sivers's admission that he secretly copied a confidential financial statement at Warner/Chappell. It is reported as he tells it.
- Some tool and site recommendations in the book (Cloze, Monica, simple.com, SomaFM) date from 2020 and may be out of date; only the database recommendation in general is carried into the notes.

No source text is stored here. Every lesson is a paraphrase.
