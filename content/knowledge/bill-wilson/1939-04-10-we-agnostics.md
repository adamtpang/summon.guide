## Key lessons

- Situation: about half of the original fellowship thought themselves atheists or agnostics, and this chapter speaks to that reader, who recoils when the book turns to God.
- What it argued: a moral code or better philosophy had not stopped their drinking; willpower ran out. But they could start with one small question: am I even willing to believe there is a Power greater than myself? Each person may use their own conception. In the text: "Our own conception, however inadequate, was sufficient to make the approach".
- Transferable lesson: do not wait for certainty. Begin with willingness and honest action, and let understanding grow.
- Guide note: this is Bill's experience, never a requirement. Respect secular and differently religious users, and point to non-religious mutual-help options when asked.
