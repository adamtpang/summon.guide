## Key lessons

- Situation: the chapter gives practical advice to a recovering person who wants to help someone still drinking, and to that person's family.
- What it argued: intensive work with other alcoholics protects the helper's own sobriety. Do not pursue someone who does not want to stop, do not lecture or brand him, and approach through a doctor or institution where possible. If he needs hospitalization he should have it, but not forcibly unless he is violent. Share your own drinking story so he can identify with it.
- Transferable lesson: the method is identification, not persuasion. In the text: "Don't start out as an evangelist or reformer."
- Guide note: the family is told to be patient and not over-anxious and to involve a doctor. Nothing here tells a layperson to manage withdrawal at home.
