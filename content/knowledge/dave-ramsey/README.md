## Caveats

- These are Ramsey Solutions marketing and education pages. Two are bylined to George Kamel, a Ramsey personality, not Dave Ramsey himself; the Baby Step pages are unsigned. Notes attribute teaching to Ramsey or his organization accordingly.
- The final bullet of each note, marked 'Limitation (not from the page...)', is NOT from the Ramsey source. It reports a mainstream disagreement and its sources are listed under limitationSources. The guide should not cite that bullet as Ramsey's teaching.
- Baby Step 4 page as read does not state a 12 percent return. The roughly 11 percent figure in note 006 is the note writer's own arithmetic from the page's $250 a month to $1.2 million example, assuming retirement at 65. The Forbes article body was not readable, so only its title supports the citation; verify before relying on it.
- The CFPB credit-building citation and the White Coat Investor return criticism were confirmed only from search excerpts.
- The pages promote Ramsey products (EveryDollar, SmartVestor Pro referrals, Financial Peace University). Notes mention this only where needed and make no product or investment recommendation. The fund categories in note 006 are reported as what the page teaches, not as advice.
- The pages include religious framing (giving 10 percent first, trusting God). Notes report the 10 percent giving line neutrally.
- Figures such as '37% of Americans cannot cover a $400 emergency' appear on the Step 1 page with a footnote that was not checked and were left out of the notes.
- Each note has one more bullet than the brief's six to nine because the task required a limitation bullet in addition; notes 001, 002, 004, 006 and 007 run to ten or eleven bullets.

No source text is stored here. Every lesson is a paraphrase.
