## Key lessons

- Russian roulette pays most players once, but repeated exposure ends in death; risks that can end the game should be avoided even at low odds, because small exposures add up. [14:21 to 15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lm9LP3uUXNs&t=861s)
- Courage and caution are not opposites: take aggressive bets with big upside and limited downside, and do not confuse volatility with ruin. A reckless gamble with a family depending on you is not courage. [15:31 to 16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lm9LP3uUXNs&t=931s)
- He treats himself as antifragile, like a muscle: moving to a country where he knew no one, speaking in public, and approaching strangers were small chosen stresses that made him tougher. [1:32 to 3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnIP39miSs&t=92s)
- Do not be the turkey: years of steady pay can hide fragility, as with long-tenured employees whose skills only fit one employer. A side skill, cash savings, and not upgrading your lifestyle with every raise are the defense. [10:00 to 11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qmab27g1zcY&t=600s)
- He sees a commission-based sales job as less fragile than a fixed-salary role that automation could erase, because every dip in income is feedback that forces you to adapt. [5:33 to 6:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dUxG43_po5s&t=333s)
- Think by subtraction first: removing distractions or bad habits usually beats adding a new trick. [12:12 to 12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dUxG43_po5s&t=732s)
- Not all success deserves admiration: many people who got rich took foolish risks and survived, while slower, steadier people kept control of their lives. [10:29 to 11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnIP39miSs&t=629s)
- The wolf and the dog: comfort bought with a collar is still a collar. He chose sales for its tight feedback loop and moved to New York partly to take a bigger swing. [7:00 to 9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lm9LP3uUXNs&t=420s)
- Trust practitioners who carry the downside of their decisions over polished people with only upside, whether in companies, politics, or expertise. [1:11 to 3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lm9LP3uUXNs&t=71s)
