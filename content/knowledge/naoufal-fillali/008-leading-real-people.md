## Key lessons

- From Machiavelli he takes realism, not cynicism: people follow incentives and protect their turf, so expect some to leave or let you down, and be pleasantly surprised by the generous ones. [1:32 to 2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRgxDxaXlEY&t=92s)
- He did not feel ready to lead until he was the top performer in the role he would manage, so the team knew he would not ask what he would not do. [2:43 to 3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRgxDxaXlEY&t=163s)
- People judge a leader by who they hire; he treats reading character in interviews as a core skill. [4:19 to 5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRgxDxaXlEY&t=259s)
- Fear gets the minimum; public praise, reward, and careful feedback get people's best. The exception is a toxic team member who damages morale, who should go quickly. [7:05 to 8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRgxDxaXlEY&t=425s)
- Keep drilling the team when things are going well, because that is when skipping training is most tempting. [8:40 to 9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRgxDxaXlEY&t=520s)
- Leaders should read history and the humanities; broad reading helps you spot patterns and avoid avoidable mistakes. [9:50 to 11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRgxDxaXlEY&t=590s)
- From Munger: get incentives right first. FedEx fixed slow night shifts by paying per shift instead of per hour. [1:56 to 3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPVaPWWe6Fo&t=116s)
- Liking someone can make you hire charm over competence and then resist admitting the mistake. [3:30 to 4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPVaPWWe6Fo&t=210s)
- Review past wins for luck before a new venture, and list the dangers the new venture has that the old ones did not. [8:55 to 10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPVaPWWe6Fo&t=535s)
- Small observed details can matter more than big projects: listening to his team's calls, he found objections came in the first thirty seconds, changed one sentence of the script, and booked meetings nearly doubled. [3:03 to 4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gALOwezukvk&t=183s)
- Following Rory Sutherland, he stresses that in business the opposite of a good idea can also be good, and that noticing real behavior beats spreadsheets alone. [1:32 to 3:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gALOwezukvk&t=92s) and [10:06 to 11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gALOwezukvk&t=606s)
