## Key lessons

- Following Albert-László Barabási, he separates performance, which is what you do, from success, which is how others see and reward it. [1:34 to 2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUUkCbaJ3hw&t=94s)
- Where performance cannot be measured cleanly, as in art, network decides a lot; he contrasts Basquiat's relentless networking with a collaborator of similar talent who stayed obscure. [2:21 to 4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUUkCbaJ3hw&t=141s)
- Performance has a ceiling but rewards do not, so the top earns hugely more for a small edge; that also means stars are closer to you in skill than their fame suggests. [4:17 to 6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUUkCbaJ3hw&t=257s)
- Starting out under someone with an established name lets their reputation rub off, as his time as a book agent did, but at some point you branch out on your own. [8:58 to 9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUUkCbaJ3hw&t=538s)
- Persistence raises the odds of a breakthrough at any age, but if you keep failing, check whether the field fits your abilities before trying harder. [9:43 to 10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUUkCbaJ3hw&t=583s)
- A few decisions made most of the difference for him, above all moving from Casablanca to Paris for an internship; he warns against getting so buried in routine work that you miss these openings. [13:51 to 14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qmab27g1zcY&t=831s)
- Choose your city deliberately, based on your field and the encounters it makes likely, and give that choice months rather than days. [4:26 to 5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HpFEbgAxm9s&t=266s)
- Wandering without a plan, in travel and in careers, leaves room for encounters and discoveries that tight schedules squeeze out. [4:59 to 5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnIP39miSs&t=299s) and [3:11 to 5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dUxG43_po5s&t=191s)
- Walkable, lively cities feed that kind of chance; he compares long energizing walks in the old town of Tangier with short, tiring ones in car-built places. [1:39 to 4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HpFEbgAxm9s&t=99s)
