## Key lessons

- He disliked books until his early twenties and later realized he did not hate reading, only being told what to read. [1:55 to 2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=115s) and [5:04 to 5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=304s)
- He started with a floor of ten pages a day and always kept a book or audiobook with him, reading in the dead minutes that would otherwise go to scrolling; that got him through about twenty books the first year. [3:54 to 5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=234s)
- Reading was tied to a concrete goal, getting an opportunity to move from Morocco to Paris, and he credits it with helping him land the internship. [2:42 to 3:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=162s)
- Go deep and broad: his sales work improved as much from psychology, economics, and military strategy as from sales books, and wider reading made him less deluded about how uncertain the world is. [7:27 to 8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=447s)
- The Lindy effect is his filter: a book that has lasted a long time is likely to last longer, so he favors Montaigne, Machiavelli, and Marcus Aurelius over fresh bestsellers, many of which he says ride marketing. [9:47 to 11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=587s)
- He uses the same test on diets and new claims, noting that fasting has lasted for millennia. [3:48 to 4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnIP39miSs&t=228s)
- An anti-library of unread books is a reminder of how much you do not know, not a source of guilt; he deliberately grows his. [12:25 to 13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnIP39miSs&t=745s) and [11:55 to 12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qmab27g1zcY&t=715s)
- He rereads Taleb's books every year and says each reread reveals something new because life experience has caught up. [0:46 to 1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2PnIP39miSs&t=46s)
- He follows curiosity from book to book instead of a set list, even when others say his interests are off topic for his job. [0:45 to 1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B3eMvNuYglc&t=45s)
- He cites research linking book reading with a longer life, as one reason among several to read. [0:45 to 1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I&t=45s)
