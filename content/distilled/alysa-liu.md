# Alysa Liu

Decide whether to pause, return, or change the terms of a demanding pursuit.

## Starting principles

### 1. Stepping away once the goals were met: retirement as graduation

In April 2022 Liu told U.S. Figure Skating she had entered the sport with two goals, had met both, and so chose to stop. She framed leaving as finishing something, not giving something up.

[Source](https://usfigureskating.org/news/2022/4/19/figure-skating-alysa-liu-graduates-from-skating-into-retirement-after-world-championships-bronze.aspx)

### 2. Testing a return in small steps and setting her own schedule

Liu told U.S. Figure Skating in October 2024 that her comeback began as curiosity, not a plan. She tried one session, then another weeks later, and only committed once she saw she could skate and study at the same time, on a schedule she controls.

[Source](https://usfigureskating.org/news/2024/10/7/rinkside-older-wiser-alysa-liu-returns-to-competition)

### 3. From all in to all out to a healthy medium

After winning the 2025 world title, Liu told CNN Sports she had lived both extremes, total immersion in skating and total disconnection from it, and that the comeback works because she now sits between them.

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2025/04/06/sport/alysa-liu-figure-skating-spt)
