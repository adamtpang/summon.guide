### 2. Odd Couple and The Dropout

Jobs and Wozniak's partnership was forged not on computers but on pranks and phone-system hacking, a template of Wozniak's engineering genius paired with Jobs's instinct to package, price, and sell it, while Jobs simultaneously immersed himself in Zen Buddhism and psychedelics at Reed College.

Source record: Odd Couple and The Dropout

### 3. Atari and India

Jobs talked his way into a night-shift job at Atari despite hygiene complaints that nearly got him fired, then used Atari's money to fund a seven-month pilgrimage to India seeking a guru, returning not enlightened in the way he expected but converted to the idea that intuition could be more powerful than Western rational analysis.
