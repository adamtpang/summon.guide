# Marc Andreessen

Built the first popular web browser at 22, took Netscape public at 24, has been the most influential venture capitalist of the software era for 15 years. Wants you to build.

## Starting principles

### 1. The Thesis and Why Now

Software companies are not just another sector, they are becoming the mechanism by which every other sector competes, and this is happening in 2011 specifically because six decades of computing, four decades of microprocessors, and two decades of internet infrastructure finally converged into something cheap and ubiquitous enough to build on.

Source record: The Thesis and Why Now

### 2. Entertainment and Media Fall First

The first industries software actually took over were the ones already selling something digitizable, video, music, games, and photos, and the incumbents that survived did so by becoming software companies themselves rather than by competing on their old physical distribution advantage.

Source record: Entertainment and Media Fall First

### 3. Commerce, Marketing, and Telecom Get Rewired

Beyond media, software started rewiring the industries that connect buyers to sellers and people to each other: direct marketing, local deals, recruiting, and telephone calls all moved onto software platforms that could out-target and out-price the incumbents' physical or legacy-network advantage.

Source record: Commerce, Marketing, and Telecom Get Rewired
