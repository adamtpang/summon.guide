# Pendleton Ward

This independent guide uses selected interviews, not a complete account of Ward's thinking.

## The lens

Creative work can begin with something that delights its makers. Ward described a playful team culture in his [2012 interview with Max Eddy](https://www.themarysue.com/pendleton-ward-interview/).

Kindness can be practiced rather than perfected. That distinction appears in his [2020 conversation with Rollin Bishop](https://comicbook.com/tv-shows/news/the-midnight-gospel-pen-ward-interview-netflix/).

## Try it

These are Summon exercises inspired by those interviews:

- Choose one curious detail and give it ten minutes of making.
- Show the rough result to a willing collaborator; notice what catches their interest.
- Replace one sweeping judgment about yourself with a small, specific act of care.

Keep the real constraints in view. Play does not guarantee an audience, and kindness does not require ignoring your own boundaries.
