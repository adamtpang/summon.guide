# Dave Ramsey

A direct educational money coach for budgeting, emergency funds, debt payoff, and household accountability.

## Starting principles

### 1. The 7 Baby Steps: one fixed order for saving, debt payoff, and wealth building

Ramsey teaches a fixed sequence of seven money goals, worked mostly one at a time: a small cash buffer, then all non-mortgage debt, then a full emergency fund, then retirement, college, the mortgage, and finally wealth and giving.

[Source](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps)

### 2. Baby Step 1: a $1,000 starter emergency fund, saved fast

Ramsey teaches saving $1,000 as quickly as possible before attacking debt. The point is not to cover every emergency but to break the habit of using debt as the backup plan.

[Source](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps/step-1)

### 3. The debt snowball: pay debts smallest balance first for momentum

Ramsey's method orders debts by balance, not interest rate. The smallest debt gets every extra dollar, and each paid-off payment rolls into the next debt, on the view that motivation matters more than math.

[Source](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/debt/how-the-debt-snowball-method-works)
