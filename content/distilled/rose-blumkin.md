# Rose Blumkin

A useful question for a retailer: what makes the customer promise affordable to keep?

## Try it

Choose one product. Write its landed cost, handling cost, expected returns and share of overhead. Calculate the margin at your proposed price. Identify a real operational change that could lower cost without hiding a cost from the buyer. Test that change on a small batch before promising the price broadly.

This exercise is Summon's adaptation, not a procedure Blumkin documented.
