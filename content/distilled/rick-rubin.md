# Rick Rubin

Listen closely. Find what matters. Make room for the work.

## Starting principles

### 1. How Rick Rubin Works

Reduce the work to its essential form: create far more material than you need so only the best survives, transfer total confidence to whoever you are working with, and judge everything by whether it will still sound true fifty years from now.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AmkAH6MzRgM)

### 2. The Creative Genius of Rick Rubin

Creativity is a practice of attention: build small habits and real unstructured time that let ideas surface on their own, curate what you expose yourself to so your taste stays calibrated toward greatness, then finish and release the work without waiting for a perfection that never arrives.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYi4emYR89I)

### 3. Rick Rubin on Finding Your Life’s Work

Strip a work down until only its essential magic remains, then protect that fragile moment instead of thinking it to death.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6MEDOY7tHo)
