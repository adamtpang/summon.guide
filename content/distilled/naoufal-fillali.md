# Naoufal Fillali

This independent guide is built from his 23 public YouTube videos as of September 2026. It is an AI summary, not his words, and he has not reviewed it.

## The lens

Earn your living where outcomes are spread fairly evenly, and place creative bets where one hit can pay for everything, only with money you can lose. He runs his own life this way, with a tech sales career and a channel on the side, in [If You're A Creator, Do Not Quit Your Job](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LO5tAzkcEeY).

Much of what we want is copied from people near us. Naming your models, choosing better ones, and making time alone helps separate borrowed wants from your own, as he describes in [We think we have free will...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QyF1ewvwsiQ).

Old books, read daily in small doses, are his main tool for both, explained in [Reading adds years to your life](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkLTZyCae2I).

## Try it

These are Summon exercises inspired by those videos:

- Write down your safe side and your speculative side. If there is no safe side, fix that before any big leap.
- List three things you want, and next to each, the person you first saw wanting it.
- Carry one book everywhere this week and read ten pages a day in the gaps.
- Start one conversation with a stranger each day for a week, in a place where people who share your interests gather.
