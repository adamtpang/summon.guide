# Leonardo da Vinci

Use close observation to connect ideas across disciplines.

## Starting principles

### 1. Train the eye first: Leonardo on what a painter must see and keep

Leonardo treats painting as a discipline of looking. The painter gathers forms directly from nature, records them before memory loses them, and builds skill one detail at a time instead of copying other painters.

[Source](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27162666M)

### 2. The body measured and opened: proportion, balance and anatomy

Leonardo studies the human body from the outside by measurement and from the inside by dissection. He wants the painter to know why a limb looks as it does, and he draws each part from several sides so it can be understood whole.

[Source](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27162666M)

### 3. Light and shade: Leonardo sorts shadows into kinds and tests them

Leonardo treats shadow as something with rules. He defines it, divides it into types, reasons about it with geometry, and checks his claims against what he sees in windows, waves, lightning and his own mistakes.

[Source](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL27162666M)
