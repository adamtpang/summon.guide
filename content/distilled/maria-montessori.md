# Maria Montessori

A physician who watched young children closely and built a way of teaching around their independence and attention.

## Starting principles

### 1. The Montessori Method, ch. III, Inaugural Address at a Children's House, 1912

A child's growth depends on the home and community around them.

[Source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/39863/pg39863-images.html)

### 2. The Montessori Method, ch. IV, Pedagogical Methods Used in the Children's Houses, 1912

Give children child-sized tools and freedom so they can do things themselves.

[Source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/39863/pg39863-images.html)

### 3. The Montessori Method, ch. V, Discipline, 1912

Real discipline is self-mastery, grown through freedom, not enforced stillness.

[Source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/39863/pg39863-images.html)
