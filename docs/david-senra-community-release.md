# David Senra community release path

This is a release kit, not permission to contact David or publish as an official
Founders product. Adam performs any outreach or posting himself.

## Positioning

**Founders Lens is a working demonstration of what Summon can add around an
expert corpus: cited cross-source reasoning, project context, installable guide
skills, and access from existing AI clients.**

It should be shown as an independent prototype over selected public-episode
syntheses, not as a Sage replacement. The partnership proposition is that David
keeps ownership and editorial control while Summon makes an authorized guide
portable across ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, and the web.

## Release gates

1. David or the rights holder approves corpus use, product naming, and community
   distribution in writing.
2. Private notes/highlights remain private, access-controlled, removable, and
   excluded from model training by downstream providers where policy allows.
3. A held-out evaluation set tests retrieval, citations, counterexamples,
   abstention, and advice boundaries.
4. The corpus owner can see source freshness, remove a source, and inspect the
   most-used questions without seeing private user context.
5. Twenty invited listeners complete a no-charge pilot before paid activation.
6. The launch page states what material is included, what is not, and who owns it.

## Pilot measures

- Percentage of answers with verified supporting sources.
- Percentage of sessions that produce a stated decision or next action.
- Helpfulness rating by question category.
- Failed retrieval and abstention rate.
- Seven-day return rate among invited listeners.
- Source-owner correction and removal turnaround time.

## Copy-ready private introduction

> I built an independent prototype that lets a founder search selected public
> Founders episode syntheses, compare historical precedents, and inspect citations.
> It does not use or reproduce your private notes and I am not presenting it as
> official. I would like to show you the prototype and explore whether an
> authorized version could make your corpus available as a portable, owner-
> controlled guide across the AI tools your community already uses.

## Launch sequence after approval

1. Private owner review.
2. Invite-only listener pilot.
3. Fix retrieval and source gaps.
4. Jointly approved naming and landing copy.
5. Community release with a visible feedback channel.
6. Paid access only after retention and source quality are demonstrated.
