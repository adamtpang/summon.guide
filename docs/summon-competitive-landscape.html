Research, 17 August 2026

Summon competitive landscape

Question: can a monthly membership for researched AI guides solve life problems better than generic AI chat or a celebrity-character app?

Conclusion: the historical-character category is crowded. The open wedge is a life-decision system: route a user’s problem to the right guide, ground the response in visible sources, turn it into an actionable plan, and retain progress over time. Sell the outcome and theme, not access to a named character.

What is crowded, what is open

  • Crowded: historical-figure chat, generic personas, generic AI companionship, and bank-connected finance apps.
  • Moderate: multi-advisor decision products.
  • Open enough to test: a non-technical “personal council” that starts with a life problem, uses researched figures as lenses, shows provenance, and produces a persistent plan.

Recommended wedge

  1. Start with Money. Promise “a money plan you can follow this week,” not a Dave simulation. Use a short intake, budget/debt decision tree, citations, scripts and a saved plan.
  2. Sell one membership, not named-guide rentals. A user gets all guides, but receives a monthly allowance of decision sessions. A council response costs multiple sessions. This aligns spend with model cost.
  3. Use guide cards as a discovery mechanic. Original pixel-art cards and theme rooms make the roster memorable, but the purchase page leads with the problem and outcome.
  4. Build a provenance ledger. Every paid answer should expose its source anchors and confidence boundaries. This is the product proof generic character apps lack.
  5. Keep paid intelligence server-side. Local skills and MCPs are convenience adapters. Retrieval, citations, premium workflows and access control remain on Summon.

First validation experiment

Ship one Money Room landing page with a free three-session trial. Measure: completion of intake, first plan generated, plan saved, return within seven days, and conversion to a founding membership. Do not expand into love or a broad all-access catalog until this loop produces paid retention.
