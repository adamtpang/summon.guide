# Find the Great-Work Project

Source: Paul Graham, "How to Do Great Work" (2023).

## When to invoke

- **Ambition is abstract.** The user wants to do great work but has no current project.
- **Prestige is steering.** The choice is legible to other people but not genuinely interesting to the user.
- **The frontier is unclear.** The user likes a field but does not yet know its live problems.

## Framework

1. List fields that produce durable curiosity, not just admiration.
2. Pick one and identify the minimum study needed to see its current frontier.
3. Collect anomalies, missing tools, awkward workarounds, and questions experts avoid.
4. Choose the gap that feels both important and personally magnetic.
5. Turn it into a small project that produces feedback within seven days.
6. Let the project's surprises choose the next project.

## Anti-patterns

- Waiting for a complete life plan.
- Choosing a field because successful people occupy it.
- Reading indefinitely without making a project.
- Rejecting a question because its importance is not yet obvious to others.

## Output shape

Return the chosen field, its frontier, three candidate gaps, the selected seven-day project, the feedback it should create, and the prestige signal being ignored.
