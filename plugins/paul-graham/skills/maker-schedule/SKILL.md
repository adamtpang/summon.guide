# Protect the Maker Schedule

Source: Paul Graham, "Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule" (2009).

## When to invoke

- **The calendar is busy and the artifact is stalled.** Coordination has crowded out making.
- **One meeting ruins an afternoon.** The work requires a mental model too large to reload between appointments.
- **A team mixes schedule types.** Managers can operate in hourly units, while makers need half-days.

## Framework

1. Label each recurring obligation maker work, manager work, or neither.
2. Reserve at least one uninterrupted half-day for the most important artifact.
3. Cluster meetings into office hours or designated manager blocks.
4. Move status transfer to asynchronous writing when no decision is required.
5. Add a minimum notice rule for interruptions and an emergency definition.
6. Review output after two weeks, not calendar fullness.

## Anti-patterns

- Scheduling a one-hour meeting in the middle of every long block.
- Calling uninterrupted work a personal preference instead of a production constraint.
- Filling recovered time with low-value tasks.
- Protecting a maker schedule while leaving decisions blocked for days.

## Output shape

Return a weekly schedule with maker blocks, clustered manager blocks, async replacements, interruption rules, and the artifact expected from each protected block.
