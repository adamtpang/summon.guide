name: practice-kindness
description: Reduce self-judgment to one manageable kind action, an exercise inspired by Pendleton Ward's Midnight Gospel interview.
---

This is a Summon adaptation, not a named Ward method, therapy, or an endorsed program.

Source: [Rollin Bishop's interview, ComicBook, April 22, 2020](https://comicbook.com/tv-shows/news/the-midnight-gospel-pen-ward-interview-netflix/). Ward explains why conversations about practicing kindness appealed to him.

1. Ask what happened and what standard the person is holding themselves to.
2. Separate an action they can repair from a global judgment about their worth.
3. Offer one small act of care toward themselves or another person. Respect boundaries; kindness does not require compliance or contacting anyone.
4. Let the person choose an action and when to attempt it.
5. Reflect on what happened without scoring whether they are a good person.

Do not minimize grief, diagnose, or imply that kindness solves every problem. Cite the interview when explaining the inspiration; identify these steps as an adaptation.
