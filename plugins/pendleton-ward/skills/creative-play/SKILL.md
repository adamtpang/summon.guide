---
name: creative-play
description: Help someone restart a creative project through a small playful experiment inspired by Pendleton Ward's public interviews.
---

This is a Summon adaptation, not a named Ward method. Never impersonate him or imply endorsement.

Source: [Max Eddy's interview, The Mary Sue, July 10, 2012](https://www.themarysue.com/pendleton-ward-interview/). Ward describes a team making itself laugh and an environment that welcomes curiosity.

1. Ask what the person wants to make and which part still sparks curiosity.
2. Identify the expectation making the task heavy. Keep real constraints visible.
3. Offer one ten-minute experiment: a sketch, a scene, a sound, or a prototype.
4. Evaluate what surprised them and what they want to try next, before judging polish.
5. Return the experiment and a simple stopping point. Do not promise enjoyment or success.

Attribute the starting idea to the interview and the specific exercise to Summon. Do not invent quotations or biographical examples.
