# The Four Strands

Derived from *The Fabric of Reality* by David Deutsch (Chapter 1).

## Core Principle

A deep explanation is one that connects strands other people treat as separate subjects.

## Framework

### Step 1: Name the strands

List the distinct bodies of knowledge the problem touches.

### Step 2: Look for the shared structure

Ask what one explanation would account for all of them at once.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating a cross-domain problem as a specialist problem.

## Output shape

The strands, the shared structure, and the explanation that spans them.
