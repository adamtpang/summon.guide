# Who The Replicator Actually Serves

Derived from *The Beginning of Infinity* by David Deutsch (Chapter 4, Creation (the neo-Darwinian selection argument, extended from genes to memes)).

## Core Principle

Deutsch's point, following neo-Darwinian theory, is that natural selection optimizes only for whichever variant is best at getting itself copied relative to its rivals, never for the good of the species, the organism, or even the whole genome, and can favor variants that actively harm the thing carrying them. He extends the same logic to memes: an idea, ritual, or corporate practice can spread and persist purely because it is good at replicating itself, independent of whether it benefits the people who carry it. Persistence and popularity are evidence of good replication, not evidence of benefit to the host.

## Framework

### Step 1: Separate the two questions

Ask separately whether this practice spreads and survives well, and whether it actually helps the people carrying it. Do not let a yes to the first stand in for a yes to the second.

### Step 2: Trace the costs and the benefits

Identify who pays to keep the practice going and who or what actually gains when it keeps spreading.

### Step 3: Run the counterfactual

Ask whether the practice would keep spreading even if it stopped helping anyone. If yes, that is the tell that it is replicator first, host second.

### Step 4: Decide what to do with the incentive

Now that the two questions are separated, decide whether to keep the practice, cut it, or change whatever is rewarding its spread.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating 'everyone does it' or 'it kept spreading' as proof of merit
- Assuming a tradition, meeting, or process must be helping you because it has survived a long time
- Fixing the symptom of a practice that is good at spreading but bad for its host, without changing what rewards the spread

## Output shape

A one line verdict on the practice under review: replicator serving, host serving, or both, plus the one incentive change that would fix it if it is replicator serving.
