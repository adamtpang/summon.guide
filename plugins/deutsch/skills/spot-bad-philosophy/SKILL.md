# Spotting Bad Philosophy

Derived from *The Beginning of Infinity* by David Deutsch (Chapter 12, A Physicist's History of Bad Philosophy (the Copenhagen interpretation, logical positivism)).

## Core Principle

Deutsch defines bad philosophy as philosophy that is not merely false but actively prevents the growth of other knowledge, and his diagnostic is specific: it characteristically declares some domain of questioning illegitimate rather than answering it, as he shows with the Copenhagen interpretation of quantum theory and logical positivism. This is different from an explanation simply being wrong. A wrong explanation can be corrected once someone asks the next question. Bad philosophy is built to resist that question in the first place, no matter how useful or well entrenched it currently looks.

## Framework

### Step 1: State the claim plainly

Write down the rule, policy, or dismissal exactly as it was given to you, without softening it.

### Step 2: Ask the next why

Ask one more why question about the claim, specifically enough that it could in principle be answered.

### Step 3: Watch how the defense responds

Notice whether the response is a better explanation, or an appeal to stop asking: authority, tradition, that is just how it works, or ridicule of the question itself.

### Step 4: Name the verdict

If the response forecloses the next question rather than answering it, treat the claim as bad philosophy regardless of how correct or useful it currently is, and go looking for the real explanation underneath it.

## Anti-patterns

- Accepting that is just how it is as an explanation instead of noticing it forecloses the next question
- Confusing a claim's usefulness, such as making correct predictions, with it being a real explanation open to improvement
- Punishing the person who keeps asking why instead of examining what the resistance to the question is protecting

## Output shape

A verdict on the claim under review: real explanation open to criticism, or bad philosophy immune to it, plus the specific question its defenders dodged.
