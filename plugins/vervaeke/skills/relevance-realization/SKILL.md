# Relevance Realization

Derived from *Zombies in Western Culture: A Twenty-First Century Crisis* by John Vervaeke, Christopher Mastropietro and Filip Miscevic (Awakening from the Meaning Crisis, lecture series).

## Core Principle

The world offers combinatorially explosive possibility and a finite mind cannot search it. No rule can specify which rules to apply. What people actually do is continuously reshape what stands out to them. That process is self-organizing and it can be trained, which means attention is a skill and not a personality trait.

## Framework

### Step 1: Stop trying to be comprehensive

Any strategy that requires evaluating all the options is not a strategy. Notice where you are attempting exhaustive search and abandon it deliberately.

### Step 2: Ask what is currently salient and why

Write down what has been occupying you. Then ask what made those things stand out. Recency, anxiety, and social pressure shape salience far more than importance does.

### Step 3: Change the conditions, not the effort

You cannot force salience by willpower. You change it by changing environment, company, practice, and what you repeatedly participate in.

## Anti-patterns

- Trying to solve an attention problem by concentrating harder.
- Building a more complete list when the problem is that lists do not rank themselves.
- Assuming what feels urgent is what matters.

## Output shape

What is currently salient, what made it salient, and the change in conditions that would reshape it.
