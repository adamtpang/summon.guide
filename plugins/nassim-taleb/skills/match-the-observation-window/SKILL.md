# Match Your Observation Window to Your Real Signal to Noise Ratio

Derived from *Fooled by Randomness* by Nassim Nicholas Taleb (Chapter Three, Philostratus in Monte Carlo: On the Difference Between Noise and Information, reinforced in Chapter Twelve, Gamblers' Ticks and Pigeons in a Box).

## Core Principle

The ratio of noise to real signal in any volatile series explodes as the observation interval shrinks. Watching a positive expectancy process every second shows almost pure noise; watching it once a year shows mostly signal. Because a loss produces a stronger emotional reaction than an equivalent gain, frequent observation of a winning but volatile process still produces a net emotional deficit. The fix is not more willpower, it is a structural change: widen the review interval to match the real frequency of meaningful change, and remove your own access to the noisy, high frequency version.

## Framework

### Step 1: Compute the real frequency of signal

Estimate how often the underlying process actually produces a meaningful, non reversible change, and set your review interval no shorter than that.

### Step 2: Precommit to the interval

Decide the review cadence in advance (weekly, monthly, quarterly) rather than deciding in the moment, when the urge to check will always win.

### Step 3: Remove your own access

Build a structural block, such as turning off notifications, deleting the app, or asking someone else to hold the number, rather than relying on discipline alone.

### Step 4: Separate the reading from the reacting

When you do look, write down whether the move exceeds the normal range for that time scale before deciding whether it deserves any response at all.

## Anti-patterns

- Checking a volatile number far more often than it can possibly produce real information
- Treating every daily fluctuation as equally meaningful as a long term trend
- Relying on willpower instead of removing your own access to the noisy feed
- Reacting emotionally to a move that is well within normal noise for that time scale

## Output shape

A written observation policy: the chosen review interval, the structural block put in place to enforce it, and the threshold that would count as a real signal worth acting on.
